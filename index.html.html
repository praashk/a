What is it?

Money that moves as data

Digital payments are transactions that take place electronically, without using physical cash. Instead, money is transferred through digital platforms or electronic means.

Unlike cheques

They do not depend on paper instruments, so payments are faster, more convenient and often more secure.

Part 2 · Behind the scenes

What happens in those seconds?

Your payment travels through several trusted systems before the merchant sees “paid”.

YouStart the payment
Payment appEncrypts the request
GatewayRoutes it securely

Encryption

Details are scrambled so outsiders cannot read them in transit.

Authentication

A PIN, password or fingerprint confirms the real owner.

Settlement

The bank moves the funds and keeps a record of the transaction.

Part 3 · Balance

Strengths and limits

Advantages

Fast and convenientPay in seconds, any time of day.
Safer than cashLess risk of theft than carrying notes.
Digital recordEvery transaction is kept for later checking.
Online shoppingUseful for buying goods and services online.
Cashless economyHelps promote a cashless economy.

Disadvantages

Needs internetNo network access means no payment.
Cyber-security risksDangerous if misused or if details leak.
Digital literacyNot suitable for people without digital skills.
The takeawayEducation and safe habits turn most limits into manageable risks.

Part 4 · Be smart

Pay safely

A few habits protect your money and your information.

Guard your PIN

Never share your PIN or OTP, even with someone claiming to help.

Trust the source

Scan QR codes and links only from trusted places.

Official apps

Install only genuine apps from official stores.

Double-check

Read the name and amount before confirming.

Red flags

Stop and think if you see any of these.

Unknown link or QRSent by a stranger or found somewhere random.
Asks for PIN or OTPReal services never ask for these.
Too good to be trueSurprise prizes and huge cashbacks.
Pressure to hurryScammers rush you so you do not think.
